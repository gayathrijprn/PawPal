A LITTLE REMINDER

Rescue with care.

If an animal is seriously injured, unconscious, struggling to breathe, bleeding heavily, or showing other emergency symptoms, contact a qualified veterinarian or animal rescue organisation as soon as possible. PawPal helps people connect and share information, but it does not replace professional veterinary care.
