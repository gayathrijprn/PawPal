WHERE YOUR KINDNESS GOES

Small donations can make a real difference.

During the journey from rescue to adoption, animals often need more care than people realise.

🏥

Medical Care

Veterinary consultations, treatments, medicines, wound care and emergency procedures.

🍲

Food & Nutrition

Nutritious meals and special diets for animals recovering from illness or malnutrition.

🏠

Temporary Care

Safe temporary shelter, bedding, cleaning supplies and everyday essentials.

❤️

Recovery

Rehabilitation, grooming, socialisation and other support before adoption.
