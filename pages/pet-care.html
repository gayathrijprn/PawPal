💚 Signs your pet may be doing well

  • ✓ Interested in play and surroundings
  • ✓ Comfortable resting and sleeping
  • ✓ Enjoys positive interaction
  • ✓ Shows normal appetite and activity
  • ✓ Curious about their environment

💛 Changes worth paying attention to

  • • Sudden withdrawal or hiding
  • • Unusual aggression or fear
  • • Loss of interest in normal activities
  • • Significant changes in eating or sleeping
  • • Sudden or persistent behaviour changes

Behaviour Changes

Hormonal changes may be accompanied by differences in behaviour, energy, attention or interaction.

Observe Look for changes from your pet's normal behaviour.
🏡

Extra Supervision

During heat, appropriate supervision and secure surroundings are especially important for pets that may seek a mate.

Safety Keep your pet secure and supervised.
💧

Everyday Comfort

Maintain normal routines as much as possible while giving your pet extra patience, rest and access to fresh water.

Remember Comfort and calm surroundings can help.
🩺

Know When to Ask a Vet

If something seems unusual or your pet appears unwell, veterinary advice is the safest choice.

Important Don't ignore unusual or severe symptoms.
🩺

When should you contact a veterinarian?

Seek veterinary advice if your pet shows unusual or concerning symptoms such as severe discomfort, marked lethargy, fever, unusual discharge, very heavy bleeding or a sudden significant change in behaviour.
