THE STORY

Every rescue deserves a chance.

Bruno was found alone and injured after being rescued from the roadside. He was frightened, weak and in need of immediate medical attention.

A local rescuer stepped in and arranged temporary shelter, food and veterinary care. Bruno is now safe, but his recovery still requires ongoing treatment and daily care.

Your contribution can help cover his medical expenses, nutritious food, medicines and temporary shelter while he gets stronger.

Our goal is simple: help Bruno recover completely and find a loving forever home.
